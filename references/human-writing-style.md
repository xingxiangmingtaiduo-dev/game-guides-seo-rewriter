# Human Writing Style

Use this reference for every article. The goal is not to disguise AI output. The goal is to produce useful editorial writing that sounds deliberate, specific, and appropriate to the reader.

## Build a credible voice

- Write as a knowledgeable editor helping a real player or product user make a decision.
- Preserve distinctive source details. Specific mechanics, constraints, menu names, mistakes, and tradeoffs create more credibility than extra adjectives.
- Match the audience. A beginner guide may explain basic terms; an advanced guide should move quickly and focus on decisions or edge cases.
- State limitations when they matter. A workflow can be useful without being effortless or universal.
- Never manufacture personal testing, first-person experience, quotations, user reactions, community consensus, or anecdotes that the source does not support.

## Avoid the standard AI article shape

- Open with the reader's immediate problem, a concrete situation, a useful answer, or a meaningful change. Do not announce that the article is comprehensive or say what it will explore.
- Do not force every section into the same pattern of definition, three benefits, summary sentence, and transition.
- Let adjacent ideas connect naturally. Remove decorative transitions when the heading or previous sentence already provides the connection.
- Mix concise sentences with fuller explanations. Vary paragraph length according to the idea instead of producing uniformly sized blocks.
- Use bullets only for genuine lists, choices, requirements, or steps. Keep connected reasoning in prose.
- Answer FAQ questions directly in the first sentence, then add only the context needed.
- End after the final useful point and CTA. Do not restate the complete table of contents.

## Remove stock language

Rewrite phrases such as:

- `In today's fast-paced digital world`
- `In this comprehensive guide`
- `This article will explore`
- `Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player`
- `It is important to note that`
- `Furthermore`, `Moreover`, and `Additionally` when used as routine paragraph starters
- `game-changer`, `unlock the full potential`, `seamless experience`, `take your gameplay to the next level`
- `在当今快节奏的时代`, `随着……不断发展`, `本文将深入探讨`, `无论你是新手还是老玩家`, `值得注意的是`, `总而言之`
- equivalent translated templates in Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, or other output languages

Replace them with a fact, action, consequence, or reader-specific observation. Do not swap one cliché for another.

## Keep SEO language natural

- Put the exact primary keyword in the H1 and SEO title.
- Use it in the introduction and one later high-value location when it reads naturally.
- After the topic is established, use the game name, feature name, pronouns, and close variants instead of repeating the full query.
- Do not start several headings or consecutive paragraphs with the same keyword.
- Write alt text to describe what is visible and why the image matters. Across a normal image plan, only one or two alt texts need the exact primary keyword; the rest should use accurate descriptions or natural variants.
- Prefer a lower keyword count over an awkward sentence. Search intent and topical clarity matter more than exact-match repetition.

## Make UgPhone insertion editorial

- Introduce UgPhone at the point where a concrete constraint appears, not on a fixed word count.
- Connect one capability to one problem: cloud execution to device heat, persistent sessions to disconnect risk, or multiple instances to account-management overhead.
- Explain the workflow or consequence instead of stacking benefits.
- Avoid repeating the same UgPhone value proposition in the introduction, main guide, FAQ, and conclusion.
- Keep the CTA proportional to the article. One clear invitation is enough.

## Revise in five passes

1. Check facts and remove unsupported implications.
2. Delete meta-writing, generic scene-setting, and repeated previews or recaps.
3. Replace vague claims with source-supported details, actions, tradeoffs, or outcomes.
4. Vary rhythm and remove repetitive paragraph openings, transitions, and sentence structures.
5. Read the introduction, UgPhone transition, FAQ, and conclusion as one continuous article; rewrite any section that sounds pasted from a template.
