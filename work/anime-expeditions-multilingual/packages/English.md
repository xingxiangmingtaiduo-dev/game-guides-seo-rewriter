# Anime Expeditions AFK Guide: Farm Resources and Build Units Efficiently

Anime Expeditions splits resources across several modes. Story supports summoning, challenges support trait work, and competitive play offers ranking rewards. Crafting, weapons, rerolls, advancement, and evolution also consume different materials. A useful AFK session starts with a target rather than the hardest stage. This **Anime Expeditions AFK Guide** explains the resource routes, continuous-run settings, and how UgPhone moves a tested session to the cloud without turning estimates into promises. The practical goal is consistency: a lower stage that restarts correctly is more useful than a difficult stage that stalls while nobody is watching.

## What Is Anime Expeditions and Which Modes Matter?

This Roblox tower-defense game has summonable heroes, waves, bosses, and expeditions. Story supplies gems and coins; harder stages may drop **Sprites**, evolution materials, and weapons.

## How Should You Prioritize Unit Progression?

Crafting combines Sprites into higher-grade components and eventually strong weapons. Equip useful weapons on the units that carry your regular farming team.

Trait Reroll spends Trait Crystals, while stat reroll improves the quality tiers rolled when a unit is obtained. Set a resource limit for both; repeated rolls do not guarantee an ideal result.

Duplicate mythic-or-higher units can be combined for advancement. Evolution instead consumes Sprites and character-specific materials. Prioritize the unit that keeps your chosen AFK stage consistent.

## How Do You Set Up Stable AFK Runs?

Before entering, enable **auto upgrade**. For repeated clears, also turn on **auto skip waves** and **auto replay**; otherwise the run may stop at the result screen.

<!-- SOURCE_IMAGE:13 -->

<!-- SOURCE_IMAGE:14 -->

Use low graphics, disable camera shake, and switch off unit VFX. These settings reduce visual load but do not strengthen the team.

<!-- SOURCE_IMAGE:15 -->

<!-- SOURCE_IMAGE:16 -->

Set automatic placement points inside the stage, then watch one full cycle. Confirm placement, upgrades, clear, settlement, and restart before leaving it unattended.
