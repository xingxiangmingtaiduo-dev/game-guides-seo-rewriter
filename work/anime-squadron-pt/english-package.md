# Anime Squadron AFK Guide: Auto Play, Farming, and Trait Reroll

Anime Squadron AFK is most useful when your team can already clear content consistently. This Roblox game uses a lane-battle format: your units leave one side of the map, fight enemy units on the route, and try to reach the opposing base. It is not a standard tower-defense layout where characters simply stay beside a road. Unit upgrades, abilities, team order, and reliable result screens still matter after automation is switched on.

There are two separate AFK routines in the source material. Stage or Infinite Mode farming is for Gems, coins, and equipment materials. The city-based Trait Reroll routine is for chasing better traits on a chosen unit. Both reduce repeated inputs, but they should not be treated as the same kind of farm.

## What Is Anime Squadron?

Anime Squadron lets you summon and improve units to hold a lane while advancing toward the enemy side. A high-level unit alone is not enough if the rest of the lineup cannot keep the lane stable.

For automated farming, use a stage your current team finishes reliably. Dependable clears usually beat one long attempt that collapses near the end.

## How to Farm Stages with Auto Play

Enter a map and turn on **Auto Play**. Before walking away, watch the opening waves and make sure the lineup does not need manual timing to survive.

The source estimates about six runs per hour, roughly 1,200 Gems and 12 to 18 boxes. Over 24 hours, the projection is 144 runs, 28,800 Gems, and 288 to 432 boxes. Defeats and stage duration change the real result.

## How Trait Reroll AFK Works

In the city, open the **Trait Reroll** NPC interface and use **Auto Reroll** when you want to look for a stronger trait on an important unit. This routine is different from stage farming because it has no guaranteed yield. The outcome depends on the rolls, so a longer session does not automatically mean a better trait.

Set a resource limit first so a secondary character does not consume materials needed for the team.
