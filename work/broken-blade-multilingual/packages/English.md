# Broken Blade AFK Guide: Level Up Without Wasting Materials

Broken Blade is a fast Roblox ARPG set in a shattered Norse world. Its loop is direct: defeat monsters, collect fragments and ore, recycle low-grade materials, strengthen a weapon, then challenge a harder boss. AFK farming looks simple because attacks can be repeated continuously, but weapon movement and unreliable targeting can push the character away or leave attacks hitting empty space. With a level cap of 15,000, stable short tests matter more than dramatic day-long projections. This **Broken Blade AFK Guide** explains progression, suitable attacks, source-based earnings, trading materials, and how to run a tested route on UgPhone without implying guaranteed levels or rare drops.

## What Is Broken Blade?

Broken Blade combines rapid combat, weapon crafting, and Boss Rush. Regular enemies provide basic stats, money, ore, and fragments. Useful materials feed weapon upgrades and Soul Forge, while lower-tier items can be recycled. Progress works in steps: clear one area reliably, concentrate resources on a main weapon, and only then move to a stronger boss.

## How to Level Up and Forge Weapons

Start in a zone where enemies die quickly. **Invest in one dependable weapon** instead of dividing materials across several unfinished choices.

## How to Choose a Weapon for AFK

Basic attacks and skills move the character differently. Prefer an attack with little displacement and a consistent hit area. Run it for ten minutes and watch whether the character stays near the spawn point.

Targeting is especially weak against bosses. Even a stationary skill can attack empty space, allowing the boss to counterattack. Compact monster routes are usually safer than bosses that require repositioning.
