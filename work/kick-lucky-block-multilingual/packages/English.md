# Kick a Lucky Block AFK Guide: Train Strength and Earn Coins Safely

Kick a Lucky Block turns one kick into a progression loop: train legs, launch the block, survive the triggered tsunami, and place Brainrots to make money. It has two AFK routines, but only one relies on repeated jump input. Choosing the wrong one can leave the account repeating an action that does not build the resource you need. This **Kick a Lucky Block AFK Guide** separates strength training from coin farming, explains rebirth, and puts the source’s daily projections in context instead of treating them as guaranteed income.

## What Is Kick a Lucky Block?

The goal is to kick a giant Lucky Block. Distance and height affect coins and tokens, while the kick triggers a tsunami. More strength generally supports longer kicks and better rewards.

Kicks can award a Brainrot. Place it on a platform to earn money from jumps; rarity changes its earning rate, so replacing weak units is part of progress.

## How Do Strength Training and Rebirth Work?

Weights and gym equipment add strength on each repetition. **Train before forcing weak kicks**: more strength lets the block travel farther.

When progress slows, rebirth resets current strength in exchange for a permanent percentage multiplier or access to stronger areas and equipment. Take it only when the bonus is worth rebuilding the current level.

## What Are the Two AFK Modes?

Strength training can continue without extra commands when the chosen equipment keeps repeating. Coin farming from jumps needs repeated input on the jump control. Watch several minutes to confirm the action is still registering.

The game description warns that exploits can cause bans. Use only allowed controls and do not alter the game.
